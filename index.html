An intentional little pipeline

Look closely.
Listen carefully. Place gently.

SeeLess pairs visual understanding with a precise speech timeline, so the added narration feels like it belongs in the film.

01 / PREPARE

Open the scene

Check the MP4 dimensions and duration; extract high-fidelity audio and sampled frames.

INPUT MP4 · FRAMES · AUDIO
02 / UNDERSTAND

Hear + see

Gnani AI STT marks dialogue intervals. Gemini follows actions, expressions, gestures, and space.

MODELS GNANI STT · GEMINI
03 / TIME IT

Find a real pause

A deterministic scheduler fits narration between speech boundaries, with safety padding.

GUARDRAIL ZERO DIALOGUE OVERLAP
04 / DELIVER

Give it a voice

Gnani AI Timbre TTS voices the description. FFmpeg mixes the clips and assembles accessible outputs.

OUTPUT MP4 · WAV · JSON
The project, plainly

Access belongs
inside the edit.

SeeLess is an AI-powered accessibility platform for blind and low-vision viewers. It turns meaningful visual events into concise spoken descriptions and places those descriptions where they won’t mask the film’s original dialogue.

A good description gives you the missing detail, then makes room for the movie again.
THE PROBLEM

Important gestures and reactions can be invisible to viewers who rely on audio when no audio description is available.

THE APPROACH

Combine original dialogue transcription, scene-aware visual analysis, and deterministic gap scheduling.

THE MODELS

Gnani AI STT for speech timing and Gnani AI Timbre TTS (timbre-v2.5) for spoken descriptions; Gemini for visual intelligence.

THE RESULT

An accessible MP4 with its original soundtrack, a standalone description WAV, and a timestamped transcript JSON.
